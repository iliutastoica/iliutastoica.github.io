Privacy

Privacy and use of this site

This page explains what the site stores, how its hosts handle requests, and how to contact me about privacy.

Site operator and tracking

Iliuta Stoica operates this portfolio. The application does not set cookies, run analytics or advertising trackers, or use fingerprinting. It has no contact form or account system.

Assets and external links

The portfolio serves its fonts, images, scripts, styles and optional typing audio from this domain. It does not embed third-party font services, analytics or social widgets. Following an external link opens another service, whose own privacy practices apply.

Offline browsing

Your browser saves copies of core pages and some pages and assets you open so you can use them without a connection. You can remove those copies by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings.

Link animation

With motion enabled, an internal link can briefly store its destination and click position in this tab’s session storage so the animation can finish on the next page. The site removes the entry when the next page loads.

Theme preference

When you explicitly choose a theme, the theme toggle saves that light/dark choice in your browser’s localStorage under the key theme. The application does not transmit this preference. You can clear it through your browser settings; without a saved choice, the site follows your system preference.

Web requests and hosting

GitHub Pages hosts the GitHub-hosted copy. The custom-domain deployment uses Spatiul.ro for hosting and Cloudflare for content delivery.

Delivering a page involves processing request information such as an IP address, requested URL and browser information. Infrastructure operators may keep request or security logs to operate and protect their services. These activities may involve personal data even though the application does not include analytics.

Cloudflare may set security cookies depending on the security features enabled for a request. These are separate from the application’s local theme preference. Provider-specific access-log retention periods have not yet been confirmed; use the contact options below with questions about infrastructure logs or cookies associated with your visit.

Messages and your rights

If you message me through an external service, I use your profile information and the information you send to respond. That service also handles your message under its own privacy policy.

Use the contact page for privacy questions or to exercise your applicable rights, including access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the authority in your EU country of residence or work.
