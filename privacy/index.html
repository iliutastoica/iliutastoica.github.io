Privacy

Plain-language privacy notes.

A static portfolio with no analytics or advertising trackers in its application code. These notes explain browser preferences, hosting services, web requests and email contact.

Site operator and tracking

Iliuta Stoica operates this portfolio. The application does not set cookies, run analytics or advertising trackers, or use fingerprinting. It has no contact form or account system. This does not mean that visiting a website involves no personal-data processing.

Assets and external links

The portfolio serves its fonts, images, scripts, styles and optional typing audio from this domain. It does not embed third-party font services, analytics or social widgets. Following an external link opens another service, whose own privacy practices apply.

One local preference

When you explicitly choose a theme, the theme toggle saves that light/dark choice in your browser’s localStorage under the key theme. The application does not transmit this preference. You can clear it through your browser settings; without a saved choice, the site follows your system preference.

Web requests and hosting

This site uses Spatiul.ro for origin hosting and Cloudflare for content delivery through its CDN. These infrastructure services are separate from the portfolio application.

Delivering a page involves processing request information such as an IP address, requested URL and browser information. Infrastructure operators may keep request or security logs to operate and protect their services. These activities may involve personal data even though the application does not include analytics. A provider operating the infrastructure does not, by itself, determine or remove my responsibilities as the site operator.

Cloudflare may set security cookies depending on the security features enabled for a request. These are separate from the application’s local theme preference. Provider-specific access-log retention periods have not yet been confirmed; contact me below with questions about infrastructure logs or cookies associated with your visit.

Email contact

If you email me, I process your sender address and the information you include to respond to and follow up your enquiry. Please include only information relevant to your message.

Questions and your rights

Contact me at hello@iliutastoica.ro with privacy questions or requests for access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection concerning your personal data, where those rights apply. You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, including the authority in your EU country of residence or work.
