01 · Intent

What the interface needed to do

Introduce modern payment journeys while preserving understandable transitions between legacy and new surfaces.

  • Keep entry and return paths clear across coexisting surfaces.
  • Represent registration and account progress explicitly.
  • Place service composition behind a deliberate frontend boundary.

02 · Design

Design decisions

Modernize incrementally

Controlled coexistence allows a journey to move without requiring a simultaneous replacement of every surrounding surface.

Design account transitions

Registration and account steps need explicit intermediate and recovery states.

Choose rendering boundaries

Rendering and backend-for-frontend patterns organize how interfaces consume service data.

03 · Implementation

Implementation

Led frontend architecture and delivery across legacy coexistence, registration and account flows, rendering choices, and backend-for-frontend patterns.
