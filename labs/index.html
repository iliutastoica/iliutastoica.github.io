Interface experiments

Small ideas.
Room to play.

A workshop for motion, light and responsive interfaces. Try the experiments, then explore the engineering work behind the portfolio.

These are local portfolio experiments, not reproductions of specific CodePens. Visit my CodePen archive (opens in a new tab).

01 / interactionA steady target. A little attraction.

Magnetic attraction

Move a mouse close to the button, or activate it with touch or keyboard. The visual responds while the clickable target stays in place.

  • Pointer feedback
  • Stable hit area
  • Touch and keyboard
02 / shape

A softer geometry

A layered SVG surface with a slow, breathing silhouette. Light and dark themes share the shape, with different materials.

  • Native SVG
  • Theme-aware material
  • Reduced-motion support
03 / light

A little depth.
A clearer focus.

Light in motion

Three translucent rays and an orbiting point turn a flat surface into a small spatial study. Transform animation keeps the effect lightweight.

  • CSS transforms
  • Shared theme tokens
  • Pause control
04 / lettering

Words in motion

An original SVG lettering study reveals its words in steps, with a small travelling point of light.

  • Native SVG
  • Opt-in motion
  • Static fallback
